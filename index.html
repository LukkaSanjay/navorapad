🔑 ⇄ 🔐

The Technology

The first time you enter your code, your browser derives a unique secret location ID using SHA-256.

Before saving your notes, photos, or voice recordings, your code scrambles everything into an AES-256 encrypted version.

  • ✔ Only the encrypted version is saved.
  • ✘ The original files and ✘ your code are never saved or sent.

This means only you hold the missing piece (your code) to locate and decrypt your pad back to its original form.

🗑️

Sharing & Copyright

Sharing a Navorapad means sharing a code. If you find it, you can edit it or delete it yourself anytime.

🌐

It's Everywhere

Our Simple Policy

  1. Free to use, always.
  2. We do not store your codes and we do not want to.
    • In the event our database is ever compromised, your pads remain unreadable because only you hold the codes to find and decrypt them (as long as you use unique, hard-to-guess codes).
    • Even if third parties demand access to stored records, your text, images, and audio remain unreadable because only you hold the decryption code.
  3. We don't care what you store in your pads because we don't know and we can't check. Only you hold the code.
  4. If you find a pad that contains your intellectual property, just delete it yourself. If you can find it, you can edit it.
  5. We are not responsible for the following:
    • If you use a pad with a simple or common code like '0000' or '1234' and someone else guesses and opens it.
    • If someone by pure chance enters your exact same code and accesses your pad.
    • Data loss caused by browser cache clearing, device failure, or unforeseen server/network outages.
